## Abstract

This manuscript studies geometric structures visible in Feynman periods and their behaviour under cuts, parameter differentiation and renormalization. Examples include the four-propagator banana graph, forest endpoint descent, the quadratically subtracted sunset and a finite-mass two-loop MOM renormalization group.

## Public status

This is a public working manuscript, not a published paper or an arXiv preprint. The links on this page provide access to the manuscript and supplementary material.
