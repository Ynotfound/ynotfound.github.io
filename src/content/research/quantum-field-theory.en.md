## Objective

Explore how complicated high-loop Feynman-diagram calculations can be decomposed into mathematical and software stages that can be checked independently. The focus includes integral reduction, master-integral evaluation, transcendental-function representations, pole assembly and physical projection.

## Computational principles

- Graph sets, symmetry factors and kinematic conventions must be versioned.
- Integral-reduction identities should be independently checkable.
- Laurent poles and finite parts require separate audits.
- Physical assembly must satisfy the relevant Ward identities and other consistency conditions.
- Software implementations need tests, benchmarks and reproducible computational certificates.
