## Direction of investigation

This project studies unavoidable sets, reducible configurations and discharging arguments in proofs of the four-color theorem. A particular interest is how structural arguments that exclude infinite graph classes can be combined with finite verification.

## Questions of interest

- Can dependence on large-scale computer enumeration be reduced?
- Is there a more general terminal framework connecting reducible configurations to boundary-color extension?
- Can five-terminal conjectures provide stronger structural tools?
- How can the reliability of computer-assisted proofs be improved through checkable certificates and formal verification?

Research notes distinguish proved results, claims established in the literature, computational experiments and unresolved conjectures.
