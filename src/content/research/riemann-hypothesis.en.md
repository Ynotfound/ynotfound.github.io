## Direction of investigation

This project studies the Riemann hypothesis through the explicit formula, Weil-type positivity criteria and the structure of associated quadratic forms. The emphasis is on finding positivity mechanisms that cover the full scale, specify their function domains and provide auditable error control.

## Working principles

- Distinguish equivalent reformulations from genuinely new structure and substantive progress.
- Audit domains, common orthogonal complements, Schur-complement reductions and limiting arguments.
- Finite base cases and numerical calculations verify only explicitly bounded claims; they do not replace an all-scale proof.
- Do not present an incomplete strategy as a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.

This page records the direction and public status of the work. It does not claim a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
