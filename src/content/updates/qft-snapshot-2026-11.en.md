The current workflow emphasizes explicit stage boundaries and independent evidence. Input graph sets and conventions need versioning; integral-reduction identities must be checkable; master integrals and their Laurent expansions require independent audits; and the final physical assembly must satisfy the relevant symmetries and consistency conditions.

This is a workflow snapshot, not a claim that a high-loop physical result has passed final validation.
