The current direction studies structural relations between reducible configurations and boundary-color extension, including whether general arguments can exclude infinite graph classes while leaving a clearly specified finite remainder for checkable verification.

This is a record of research questions, not a completed result. Verification of finite cases does not automatically produce a computer-free proof of the four-color theorem.
