This snapshot organizes the programme into four layers: graph families and Symanzik geometry; identification of MZV, elliptic and higher geometric structures; operations involving cuts, parameter differentiation, UV contractions and forest subtraction; and physical closure through RG functions, Ward identities and observables.

Connections between these layers still require individual proofs or checks. This entry records the organization of the programme, not a completed classification theorem.
