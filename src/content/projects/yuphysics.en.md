## What it contains

YuPhysics is the public archive for the Feynman-period research programme. It collects the main manuscript, focused papers, classification tables, LaTeX sources, proof notes, computational code and supporting certificates.

## Organization principles

Claims are organized by their level of verification, distinguishing general proofs, conditional results, numerical checks, conjectures and open problems. Manuscript snapshots from different dates are not automatically equivalent to formally published results.
